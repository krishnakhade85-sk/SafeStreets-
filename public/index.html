Our Privacy & Safety Manifesto

  • ✓
    Anonymous by Default: We never display contributor names, IP addresses, or social accounts.
  • ✓
    Automated PII Redaction: Phone numbers, email addresses, vehicle plates, and private residence mentions are automatically stripped.
  • ✓
    No Live Location Tracking: We never request or log your persistent background GPS coordinates.
  • ✓
    Aggregated Nuance: No area is permanently labeled unsafe based on a single review. We aggregate recent time-specific observations.
  • ✓
    Non-Alarmist Tone: We reject fear-mongering and victim-blaming language. The focus is practical, respectful empowerment.
